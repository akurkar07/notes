# Classroom 42 Software Engineer Internship - James Espinosa

This internship was obtained via the Nottingham Internship Scheme. Documentation Below.

## Forefront & Process

On April 1 2026, the Nottingham Internship Scheme came out for the 25/26 cohort, coming with a lot of internships - 67, to be exact. [The list is here.](../uoninternship2026.md)

I had only applied to 1 internship for the Nottingham Internship Scheme - Classroom 42's "Full Stack Developer Internship". 
I handed out my CV and Cover letter on April 12, 2026. While this was late, I managed to get through. What I had to do next was to do a project in 3 days, in which I would be tested by the web developer, Haider.

The interview was scary. (more on this soon, I'm leaving it unfinished for now)

Then, roughly at 2pm on Saturday, I get an email. The offer. I win.

If you are a first year student, it is entirely possible. I got questioned on my hackathons and what the biggest challenge was - and I said "consistency". When I say consistency, I mean consistently coding for the right goal and project - sometimes we wouldn't even talk about it until the end of the hackathon, then we realised we messed up. To combat this, I decided to talk about where the project goes every hour, as well as making sure everyone is on the same level of understanding - this way, we can whittle out what people are getting wrong and what they don't know.

Getting an internship in your first year also comes down to cold emailing on LinkedIn and/or Twitter.

Further on they would allow me to ask questions about them.
