# Coordinate Vector

## Definition

The [[Coordinate Vector|coordinate vector]] of $\mathbf{x}$ relative to a basis $B$ is the list of scalars needed to build $\mathbf{x}$ from the vectors in $B$.

If

$$
B=(\mathbf{v}_1,\dots,\mathbf{v}_n)
$$

and

$$
\mathbf{x}=c_1\mathbf{v}_1+\cdots+c_n\mathbf{v}_n,
$$

then

$$
[\mathbf{x}]_B=
\begin{pmatrix}
c_1\\
\vdots\\
c_n
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

## Why It Matters

Coordinate vectors let us work with abstract basis vectors using ordinary column vectors.

They are unique because a basis is both spanning and [[Linear Independence|linearly independent]].
