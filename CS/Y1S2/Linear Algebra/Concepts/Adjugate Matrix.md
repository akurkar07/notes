# Adjugate Matrix

## Definition

The [[Adjugate Matrix|adjugate matrix]] of a square matrix $A$, written $\operatorname{adj}(A)$, is the transpose of the cofactor matrix of $A$.

## Why It Matters

The adjugate appears in the formula for the inverse of a matrix:

$$
A^{-1}=\frac{1}{\det(A)}\operatorname{adj}(A),
$$

provided $\det(A)\neq 0$.

This formula is usually more useful theoretically than computationally.
