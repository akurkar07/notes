# Identity Matrix

## Definition

The [[Identity Matrix|identity matrix]] $I_n$ is the $n\times n$ matrix with $1$s on the main diagonal and $0$s everywhere else.

For example,

$$
I_3=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0\\
0 & 1 & 0\\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

## Why It Matters

The identity matrix acts like the number $1$ for matrix multiplication:

$$
AI_n=A
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
I_mA=A
$$

whenever the products are defined.

It is the [[Standard Matrix|standard matrix]] of the identity transformation.
