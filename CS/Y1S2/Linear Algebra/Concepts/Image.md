# Image

## Definition

The [[Image|image]] of a [[Linear Transformation|linear transformation]] is the set of all possible outputs.

If

$$
T:V\to W,
$$

then

$$
\operatorname{im}(T)=\{T(\mathbf{x})\mid \mathbf{x}\in V\}.
$$

## Why It Matters

The image tells us what part of the [[Codomain|codomain]] is actually reached by the transformation.

For a matrix transformation $T(\mathbf{x})=A\mathbf{x}$,

$$
\operatorname{im}(T)=\operatorname{Col}(A),
$$

so the image is the same as the [[Column Space|column space]] of the matrix.
