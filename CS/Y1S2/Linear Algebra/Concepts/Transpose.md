# Transpose

## Definition

The [[Transpose|transpose]] of a matrix $A$, written $A^T$, is the matrix obtained by swapping the rows and columns of $A$.

If

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3\\
4 & 5 & 6
\end{pmatrix},
$$

then

$$
A^T=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 4\\
2 & 5\\
3 & 6
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

## Why It Matters

The transpose appears in:

- dot products written as matrix products;
- [[Normal Equations|normal equations]] for least squares;
- the [[Adjugate Matrix|adjugate matrix]];
- orthogonality calculations.
