# Norm

## Definition

The [[Norm|norm]] of a vector is its length.

For $\mathbf{v}\in\mathbb{R}^n$,

$$
\|\mathbf{v}\|=\sqrt{\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{v}}.
$$

## Why It Matters

Norms measure distance and error.

In [[Least Squares|least squares]], we choose $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ to minimize

$$
\|A\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{b}\|.
$$

## Example

If

$$
\mathbf{v}=
\begin{pmatrix}
3\\
4
\end{pmatrix},
$$

then

$$
\|\mathbf{v}\|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5.
$$
