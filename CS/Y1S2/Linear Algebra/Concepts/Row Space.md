# Row Space

## Definition

The [[Row Space|row space]] of a matrix is the span of its row vectors.

For an $m\times n$ matrix $A$, the row space is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$.

## Why It Matters

The row space records the independent equations represented by a matrix.

It has the same dimension as the [[Column Space|column space]]:

$$
\dim(\operatorname{Row}(A))=\operatorname{rank}(A).
$$

The row space is also connected to the [[Null Space|null space]] by orthogonality:

$$
\operatorname{Row}(A)=(\operatorname{Nul}(A))^\perp.
$$
