## Topics Covered

- Image formation, pixels, sampling, quantisation, and image representation
- Histograms, contrast, intensity transformations, and histogram processing
- Linear filtering, convolution, smoothing, and sharpening
- Non-linear filtering and noise reduction
- Binary image thresholding and connected components
- Morphological operations such as erosion, dilation, opening, and closing
- Derivative filters, edge detection, and gradients
- The Hough transform for detecting parameterised shapes
- Segmentation, superpixels, and interactive segmentation

## Revision Focus

1. Be able to explain the image-processing pipeline from image capture to feature extraction.
2. Practise small convolution/filtering examples by hand.
3. Know when to use histogram methods, thresholding, morphology, and segmentation.
4. Compare linear and non-linear filters by their effect on noise, edges, and detail.
5. Understand what the Hough transform accumulates and why it is useful for line or shape detection.
